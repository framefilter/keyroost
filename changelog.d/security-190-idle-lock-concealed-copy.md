- **The app locks itself after 5 minutes without input.** An unlocked FIDO
  key, a remembered Authenticator password or OTP PIN, and the codes on
  screen are cleared once the window has had no mouse or keyboard input for
  5 minutes; a running operation is never interrupted. Copied codes and
  generated management keys are now marked to stay out of clipboard history
  on macOS, Windows and X11. ([#190])
