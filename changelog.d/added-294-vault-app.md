- **The password vault in the app (experimental).** A Vault tab on FIDO2 keys
  that can hold one, beside Authenticator, with a Password vault card on the
  Overview: unlock with the PIN and a touch, copy usernames and passwords (out
  of clipboard history on macOS, Windows and X11, and cleared after 45 seconds
  or when the vault locks), add and edit entries with a password generator,
  delete entries, create, delete and rotate vaults, and export and import
  backups, ticking which entries to bring in. A change made elsewhere is
  reloaded, never overwritten. A Learn page explains it. ([#294])
